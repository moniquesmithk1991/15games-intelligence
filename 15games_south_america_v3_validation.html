Method and limitations

Results are evaluated in chronological order, grouped by identical kickoff timestamps to avoid using simultaneous results. All four leagues maintain separate team histories. Team identifiers are used when present. Poisson score probabilities enumerate 0–10 goals per side and are normalized. A historical 1–1 baseline is included, alongside a training-frequency Top-5 benchmark. Coverage and skipped fixtures are reported explicitly. Repeated seasons may contain changed teams or competition structures. Before betting, validate V3 prospectively on matches not yet played and compare with bookmaker implied probabilities and fees.
