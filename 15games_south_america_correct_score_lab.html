Method

Expected home goals = league home-goals average × (home team's home scoring average ÷ league home average) × (away team's away goals conceded average ÷ league home average). Expected away goals use the corresponding away-scoring and home-conceding rates. Rates are smoothed toward league averages using three virtual matches, and expected goals are capped at 0.2–4.0. The independent Poisson model enumerates scores 0–10, renormalizes probabilities, and sorts the most likely outcomes. Model parameters have not been optimized on the test data.
