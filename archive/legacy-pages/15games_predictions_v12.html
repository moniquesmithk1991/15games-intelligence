15GAMES · Accumulator Risk Lab V11

Enter each leg's bookmaker price and an independently estimated probability. The tool calculates implied probability, model edge, individual risk and combined ticket probability. No verified model estimate? Leave the model probability blank. It will show a bookmaker-implied benchmark, not pretend it is a prediction.

Prices support decimal (1.50), American negative (-200), and American positive (+150). Risk flags are based on probabilities, not guarantees. Multiplying leg probabilities assumes independence; matches or markets may be correlated, making the ticket calculation unreliable. Bookmaker implied probabilities include margin. No real-time bookmaker data is fetched here.
