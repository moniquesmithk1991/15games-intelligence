Methodology

Each final match adds one home and one away observation. Goal averages are GF ÷ matches and GA ÷ matches. No upcoming fixtures, league tables or bookmaker odds are used. Team names are provisional identifiers; renames and aliases need normalization.

Important: the current dataset is a short rolling sample, not a full season. Averages based on one or two matches are unstable. We will not claim predictive accuracy until enough chronological history is available and a walk-forward test beats the existing baseline.
