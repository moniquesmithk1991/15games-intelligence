Method and limitations

Results are evaluated in chronological order, grouped by identical kickoff timestamps to avoid using simultaneous results. All four leagues maintain separate team histories. V5 uses smoothed weighted empirical histograms of team scoring and opponent conceding, with 240-day decay and bounded exponential tilt. This is a heuristic candidate, not a trained or calibrated machine-learning system. Team identifiers are used when present. Both models enumerate 0–10 goals per side and normalize score probabilities; V3 uses Poisson and V5 uses empirical distributions. A historical 1–1 baseline is included, alongside a training-frequency Top-5 benchmark. Coverage and skipped fixtures are reported explicitly. Repeated seasons may contain changed teams or competition structures. Before betting, validate V5 prospectively on matches not yet played and compare with bookmaker implied probabilities and fees.
