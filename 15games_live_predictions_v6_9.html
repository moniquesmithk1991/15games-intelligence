How predictions work

Uses the latest 30 historical matches per team, home/away goal rates with shrinkage toward league averages, independent Poisson exact-score probabilities, and bookmaker implied 1X2 probabilities with overround removed. This is a live-fixture MVP with exploratory V5 regional league weighting, not a validated improvement over V4. The V5 league accuracy is historical context, not a guaranteed future hit rate. Do not use these probabilities as a claim of profitability.
